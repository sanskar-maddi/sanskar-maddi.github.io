03 /A little about me

Curiosity,
made tangible.

I’m an aerospace engineer and experimental fluid dynamics researcher. I’m drawn to phenomena that happen too quickly to see — and to the challenge of building the tools that make them visible and understandable.

My interests span compressible and multiphase flows, hypersonics, shock–boundary-layer interaction, thermoacoustics, and propulsion. Across these topics, I bring together physical intuition, carefully designed measurements, and computational analysis.
