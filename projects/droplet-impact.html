The question

What can high-speed observation reveal about a liquid’s response during impact?

Project overview

During my visiting research internship at the University of Alberta, I investigated drop-impact dynamics in non-Newtonian fluids. The work used ultrafast visualization to study the transient evolution of the liquid interface.

Drop impact combines rapid deformation with the response of the fluid material. Resolving the event in time helps connect the shapes seen in an experiment with the underlying dynamics.

Approach & methods

01

Capture the transient event

Use high-speed visualization to observe rapid interface motion during impact.

02

Analyze the image sequence

Follow the evolution of the liquid interface through experimental image analysis.

03

Interpret the fluid response

Relate observed deformation to the impact dynamics of non-Newtonian fluids.

Areas of focus

  • Rapid free-surface deformation
  • Drop-impact dynamics in non-Newtonian fluids
  • Time-resolved experimental observation
